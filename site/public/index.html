The protocol

Four objects.
Three weeks to the first paid call.

Append-only ledger, deterministic policy, privacy-preserving proofs, paid APIs. Each piece is shippable on its own; together they're the resolution network.

01 The Ledger
Append-only RentEvent stream
Facts are signed events on a RentObligation. Corrections are new events; nothing is ever silently edited.
RentObligation→RentEvent→Reliability→Proof
PII never in events
Party, property, and policy are opaque IDs. The real facts live in a private vault only the renter can disclose from.
Assistance doesn't erase history
An assisted month ends an independent streak, never the satisfied record. 25/25 is still 25/25.
Reconstructible from events alone
State derives deterministically from the event stream. No hidden mutations, no side channels.
02 Policy Engine
Deterministic, landlord-authored
Agents interpret requests; explicit policy decides what's allowed. In-policy resolves instantly.
Natural language→Agent→Policy check→Resolution
Example: 60% now, 10 days, twice a year
A landlord sets it once. The agent translates "I can pay Thursday" into a policy check; the engine says yes or no deterministically.
Out-of-policy never invents approval
The agent can't fabricate landlord consent. Policy violations return no; they never become a maybe.
Borrow last, always
Resolution order is fixed: correction, then flex, then assistance, then employer benefit, then financing. Debt is the last card.
Privacy

Public proof.
Private facts.

The chain sees Merkle roots only. Everything sensitive lives off it, and disclosure is the renter's choice.

No PII onchain, ever
Names, addresses, leases, income, bank IDs — none of it. The public chain holds cryptographic roots and nothing else. Not even salted hashes of guessable facts.
Minimum-disclosure claims
Prove "12 of 12 satisfied" without revealing the address, the amounts, or the history. The verifier sees only the claim you authorized.
Tamper-evident without trust
Change a record, break the root. Anyone checking the anchor on Base sees the break. No need to trust us on history.
Earn the chain
Anchoring starts on a cheap EVM L2 (Base Sepolia, then mainnet). A permissioned network is a P3, only when multiple institutions need shared governance.

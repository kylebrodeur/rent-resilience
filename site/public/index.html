rentresilience

Reliable rent should count for something the month you need it to.

Rent Resilience is agentic infrastructure for resolving housing obligations. Rent becomes a verifiable obligation in an append-only ledger. Private facts stay offchain while cryptographic proofs anchor to Base. When a shortfall hits, agents compose the least harmful fix across landlord flexibility, assistance, and financing. Borrowing comes last.

Paying rent on time, consecutively, with no assistance should qualify you for assistance the one month you need it.

Private facts, public proofs

No PII ever touches the chain. Merkle commitments on Base make history tamper-evident; renters control what gets disclosed, to whom, claim by claim.

Agents pay for trust

Proofs, verification, and resolution quotes are paid by other agents in USDC over x402. Machine-payable trust at prices card rails cannot serve.

Open protocol

RentObligation and RentEvent schemas are published under MIT. State derives from signed events; corrections are new events, never silent edits.

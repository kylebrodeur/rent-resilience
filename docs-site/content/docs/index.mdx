> Rent should compound into financial resilience. Private facts, public proofs,
> portable trust.

Rent is most renters' largest recurring bill, and years of on-time payments earn
nothing the month something goes wrong. This protocol represents rent as a verifiable
obligation in an append-only ledger, keeps private facts off-chain, anchors proofs to
Base, and lets agents resolve shortfalls across landlord flexibility, assistance, and
financing — debt last, always.
